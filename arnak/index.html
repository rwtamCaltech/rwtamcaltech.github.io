How this works

Each month a scraper walks every tracked player's finished Arnak games on BoardGameArena, keeps their most recent 100 three-player Snake Temple games (or fewer if they have played fewer), and averages BGA's end-of-game statistics over those games.

Stats

  • PlayerELO: after the player's latest 3-player Snake game (ELO shown minus BGA's 1300 offset).
  • AverageOppELO: strength of schedule, the average pre-game ELO of the two opponents.
  • First/Second/ThirdPlaceFreq: share of games finished in each place.
  • ave_player_score: research + temple + cards + fear + idols + guardians scores. ave_opp_scores: average opponent score.
  • differential = ave_player_score − ave_opp_scores.
  • overcome/sites discovered = guardians ÷ sites-discovered.
  • total resources = (tablets + 2 × arrowheads + 3 × jewels) ÷ 10.
  • sos-pt diff aggregate = AverageOppELO1.5 × differential ÷ 10000 (0 if the differential is negative).
  • sos-pt adjusted: a boost for players who face unusually tough opponents. If a player's AverageOppELO is green (more than one SD above the average, about 398 right now), their sos-pt diff aggregate is multiplied by (AverageOppELO ÷ cutoff)1.5; everyone else keeps their sos-pt unchanged. Players who don't outscore their opponents stay at 0. This orders the 3PL Snake rank.

Colors, strengths and weaknesses

A value is green when it is more than one standard deviation above the average of all tracked players and red when more than one below. The averages change as players are added. Strength and weakness labels come from the same test on selected stats; ELITE PLAYER / SUBPAR PLAYER uses sos-pt adjusted. ELITEAVE averages the players whose sos-pt adjusted is more than one SD above average.

Nationalities

Each player's country is the one shown on their BoardGameArena profile. The country table is sorted by number of players. Its Rank column ranks countries by the average 3PL Snake rank of their players (lower is better), so a country with one strong player can rank above a large country with many mid-ranked players. Each country's best player is listed alongside.

Play comparisons

Similarity is the cosine similarity of two players' stat vectors (score-research through gained-fear), min–max normalized across all players so 1 is the selected player and 0 is the least similar. Hover a similarity to see the raw cosine value. A near-identical profile can hint at a smurf account; a low top match suggests an unusual playstyle.
